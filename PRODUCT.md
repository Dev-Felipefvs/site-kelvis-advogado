## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, published on GitHub Pages (repo próprio na conta Dev-Felipefvs). Decided by the agency (MAREME) for all client sites.

## Users

People in Maceió (especially Tabuleiro do Martins and nearby bairros) who need a lawyer: workers with a labor claim, INSS claimants (auxílio-doença, LOAS, pensão por morte, aposentadoria por invalidez, tempo de contribuição), families facing a criminal matter, people with a civil dispute. Mostly on a phone, arriving from Google Maps or Instagram, wanting to talk to the lawyer directly on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Present Kelvis Antônio da Silva (Sociedade Individual de Advocacia, OAB/AL nº 20333) and turn a visit into a WhatsApp conversation. Success = the visitor understands who he is, which areas he handles, where the office is, and starts a WhatsApp chat.

## Positioning

A solo lawyer with a street-front office in Tabuleiro do Martins, listed as open 24 hours, who attends personally. Not a large firm.

- Contact: WhatsApp/telefone (82) 99435-9080. Instagram @kelvisantonioadv.
- Areas (from the office's own facade signage): Criminal, Cível, Trabalhista, Previdenciário (auxílio-doença, LOAS, pensão por morte, aposentadoria por invalidez, tempo de contribuição).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- OAB Provimento 205/2021: informative tone, no promise of results, no mercantilização, no citing clients or cases, no comparatives/superlatives. Reviews mentioning case wins are not shown (decision confirmed by Emerson, 14/11/2026).
- Never invent numbers, years of experience, awards, clients or reviews.

## Brand Commitments

- Existing logo: "KA" monogram with scales, "KELVIS ANTÔNIO · ADVOGADO" (navy/blue version and black/gold facade version).
- Facade: black sign with gold lettering.

- Google: nota 5,0 com 6 avaliações. Usable quotes: Islane Tenório ("Ótimo espaço! Advogado simpático e competente."), Darlon Kayque ("Atendimento personalizado e muito resolutivo."), thiagolima94 ("Dr. Kelvis é um excelente profissional.").

## Product Principles

1. Direct line to the lawyer: every section leads to WhatsApp.
2. Plain language for people who are not lawyers.
3. Inform, never promise.
4. Local and reachable: the office on the avenue, open 24h.
